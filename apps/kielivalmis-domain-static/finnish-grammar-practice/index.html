Questions learners ask

How should I practise Finnish grammar?

Practise grammar inside real sentences, then use those sentences in speaking and writing.

Why is Finnish grammar difficult?

Finnish has cases, word forms and structures that may be new to learners. Regular sentence-level practice helps make them more natural.

Does KieliValmis teach grammar?

KieliValmis includes grammar support as part of practical Finnish learning, vocabulary, sentences and correction practice.

KieliValmis is a learning and practice platform. It is not an official YKI test organizer and does not guarantee exam results.
