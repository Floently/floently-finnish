Questions learners ask

How do I improve YKI speaking?

Practise short answers daily, record yourself, repeat corrected versions and train common everyday topics.

Can Floently help me speak Finnish more fluently?

Floently is designed to help learners practise speaking, correction and roleplay so Finnish becomes more usable in real situations.

Is grammar important for speaking?

Yes. Grammar matters because it helps your meaning become clear, but speaking practice should connect grammar to real sentences and situations.

Floently Finnish is a learning and practice platform. It is not an official YKI test organizer and does not guarantee exam results.
