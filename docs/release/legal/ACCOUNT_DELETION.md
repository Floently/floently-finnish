# Account Deletion Information (Public Page Draft)

Last updated: 2026-04-24

## How to delete your account
1. Open the app.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm deletion.

## What happens after deletion
- Account access tokens and sessions are invalidated.
- Account-linked personal data is removed from active application stores and linked tables where applicable.
- Some records may be retained when required by law, fraud prevention, or accounting obligations.

## Timeline
Deletion requests are normally completed within 24 hours.

## Subscriptions
Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active store subscription. Manage cancellations in:
- Apple App Store subscriptions (iOS)
- Google Play subscriptions (Android)
