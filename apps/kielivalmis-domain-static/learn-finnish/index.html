KieliValmis helps adults practise Finnish for goals that matter outside a textbook: preparing for the YKI exam, getting ready for work in Finland, understanding services, speaking with other people and handling everyday life with more independence.

Start learning Finnish with KieliValmis

Learn Finnish by using it

Build vocabulary and grammar inside complete sentences, speaking tasks, writing prompts and realistic situations. The goal is to make Finnish usable when you need to answer, ask, explain or understand.

Finnish for YKI exam preparation

Practise the four YKI skills—speaking, writing, listening and reading—and connect them to vocabulary, grammar and communication strategies that help you keep going when a task is difficult.

Finnish for work life in Finland

Prepare for workplace instructions, colleagues, supervisors, customers, meetings and professional conversations. Practise asking for clarification, reporting progress and explaining problems clearly.

Finnish for everyday life

Use practical topics such as housing, healthcare, transport, shopping, appointments, education, family and official matters to build language that supports daily independence in Finland.

Practice with guidance you understand

Questions learners ask

How can I learn Finnish for life in Finland?

Combine regular speaking, listening, reading and writing with vocabulary and grammar that appear in real situations such as work, services, healthcare, housing and everyday conversations.

Can KieliValmis help me prepare for YKI?

Yes. KieliValmis includes Finnish practice for YKI speaking, writing, listening, reading, vocabulary, grammar and realistic communication.

Can I practise Finnish for work life in Finland?

Yes. KieliValmis includes workplace Finnish and roleplay-style practice for instructions, colleagues, customers, meetings and professional situations.

KieliValmis is an independent Finnish learning and practice platform for YKI preparation, working life and everyday communication.
