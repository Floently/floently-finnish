Finnish for work life

Prepare for work in Finland with practical Finnish

Finnish for working life is more than job vocabulary. You need to understand instructions, ask questions, explain what happened, speak with colleagues, solve misunderstandings and communicate clearly with customers or patients. KieliValmis helps you practise those situations before you need them at work.

Start workplace Finnish practice

Understand workplace instructions

Practise the language of tasks, schedules, safety, tools, locations and next steps. Train how to ask someone to repeat, explain or show you something when you are not sure.

Speak with colleagues and supervisors

Useful Finnish for work includes reporting progress, saying that something is finished, explaining a delay, asking for help, giving an update and checking what should happen next.

Practise customer and professional situations

Customer-facing work often requires polite questions, clarification, problem solving and clear explanations. Roleplay helps you practise the whole interaction instead of memorising one phrase at a time.

Build professional vocabulary in context

Work vocabulary is easier to remember when it appears inside a realistic situation. KieliValmis connects words and grammar to actions, conversations and decisions that happen in working life.

Connect Finnish for work with YKI preparation

If you are also preparing for the YKI exam, workplace and everyday scenarios give you useful material for speaking, listening and vocabulary practice. The goal is one Finnish skill set that works both in the exam room and in real life.

Questions learners ask

How can I prepare my Finnish for work in Finland?

Practise common workplace actions: understanding instructions, asking for clarification, reporting progress, discussing problems, speaking with colleagues and handling customer situations.

What is workplace Finnish?

Workplace Finnish is the language used for instructions, teamwork, meetings, reporting, customer service, safety, schedules and professional communication.

Can workplace Finnish also help with YKI preparation?

Yes. Workplace situations strengthen practical speaking, listening, vocabulary and interaction skills that are also useful when preparing for YKI.
