Support

Last updated: 2026-07-24

Need help with Floently Finnish? Contact support@floently.com.

What to include

  • Your account email address.
  • The device and platform you are using: web, iOS or Android.
  • A short description of the problem.
  • Screenshots or error messages when available.

Common support topics

  • Sign-in or Google login issues.
  • Subscription or access problems.
  • YKI practice, speaking, grammar or vocabulary problems.
  • Account deletion or privacy requests.
