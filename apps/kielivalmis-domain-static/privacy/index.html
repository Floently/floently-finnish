Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-08

KieliValmis is a Floently product operated by Komplyint Oy. KieliValmis is the new customer-facing name for the Finnish-learning service previously presented as Floently Finnish. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the service on web, iOS or Android.

Information we collect

  • Account information, such as email address, name if provided, sign-in method and authentication identifiers.
  • Learning activity, such as lessons opened, practice progress, vocabulary, grammar, speaking and YKI practice results.
  • Audio or speech practice content when you choose to record or submit spoken Finnish responses.
  • Payment and subscription entitlement information from app stores or payment providers. KieliValmis does not store full card details.
  • Technical information such as device type, app version, logs, diagnostics and security events.

How we use information

  • To provide the learning service and keep your progress available across sessions.
  • To support Finnish practice, YKI preparation, feedback, roleplay and subscription access.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, fix bugs and improve the service.
  • To respond to support, privacy and account deletion requests.

Third-party services

KieliValmis may use trusted services for authentication, hosting, payments, analytics, crash reporting, app distribution, language processing and email support. These providers process data only as needed to operate the service.

Data retention and deletion

You can request deletion of your KieliValmis account and associated learning data. Some limited records may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, tax, payment or dispute purposes.

Brand transition and account continuity

The change from Floently Finnish to KieliValmis is a customer-facing rebrand of the existing service, not a transfer to a separate account system. Existing account, progress and subscription continuity remain connected to the same service infrastructure.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@floently.com.
