Delete Your Account

Last updated: 2026-08-08

You can request deletion of your KieliValmis account and associated learning data. KieliValmis is the new customer-facing name for the existing Floently Finnish learning service.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@floently.com from the email address connected to your account.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my KieliValmis account.
  3. Include the account email address and confirm that you want the account deleted.

What will be deleted

  • Your account profile.
  • Your learning progress and practice history where stored by the service.
  • Submitted practice content associated with your account where deletion is technically and legally possible.

What may be retained

Some limited records may be retained where required for legal, tax, payment, security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution reasons. App store subscription history may remain with Apple or Google according to their own rules.

Subscriptions

Deleting your KieliValmis account does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel subscriptions directly through the store where you purchased them.

Deleting the app is not account deletion

Removing the mobile app from your device does not delete your account. Use the request process above when you want the account and associated eligible data deleted.
