Learn Finnish for YKI, work and everyday life

KieliValmis helps adults build practical Finnish for real life in Finland. The focus is not only memorising words, but practising the Finnish you need for YKI, work, services, appointments, study and daily communication.

Start learning Finnish with KieliValmis

Why KieliValmis exists

Many learners need Finnish for serious life goals: passing YKI, working in Finland, applying for citizenship, communicating with employers, and handling everyday situations. KieliValmis brings these needs into one learning path.

What you can practise

You can practise vocabulary, sentence patterns, grammar, roleplay, speaking situations, YKI-style tasks and workplace Finnish. The goal is to make Finnish usable, not just theoretical.

Who it is for

KieliValmis is designed for immigrants, workers, students, healthcare professionals and adults who want structured Finnish practice for life in Finland.

Questions learners ask

What is KieliValmis?

KieliValmis is a Finnish learning app and web platform focused on YKI preparation, workplace Finnish, grammar, vocabulary, speaking practice and everyday Finnish.

Can KieliValmis help me learn Finnish for work?

Yes. KieliValmis includes workplace Finnish and roleplay-style practice for professional and everyday communication.

Is KieliValmis only for beginners?

No. KieliValmis is useful for learners who need practical Finnish for YKI, work and daily communication. It is especially useful when you already know some Finnish but need more confidence using it.

KieliValmis is a learning and practice platform. It is not an official YKI test organizer and does not guarantee exam results.
