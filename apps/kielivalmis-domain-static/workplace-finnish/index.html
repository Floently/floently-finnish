Questions learners ask

What is workplace Finnish?

Workplace Finnish is the Finnish used for instructions, teamwork, reporting, customer situations, meetings and professional communication.

Can KieliValmis help with Finnish for work?

Yes. KieliValmis includes practical Finnish and roleplay-style practice for working life in Finland.

Is workplace Finnish useful for YKI?

Yes. Work and everyday topics often strengthen the communication skills learners need for YKI-style Finnish.
