Questions learners ask

How can I practise for YKI Finnish?

Practise speaking, writing, reading, listening, vocabulary and grammar regularly. KieliValmis gives guided practice and roleplay situations to help you use Finnish more confidently.

Does KieliValmis replace the official YKI test?

No. KieliValmis is not an official YKI organizer and does not replace the official test. It is a practice platform for learners preparing to use Finnish in YKI-style and real-life situations.

Can I practise YKI speaking with KieliValmis?

Yes. KieliValmis is built around practical speaking, roleplay and correction-oriented training for Finnish communication.

KieliValmis is a learning and practice platform. It is not an official YKI test organizer and does not guarantee exam results.
