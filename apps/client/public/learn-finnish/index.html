Learn Finnish for YKI, work and everyday life

Floently Finnish helps adults build practical Finnish for real life in Finland. The focus is not only memorising words, but practising the Finnish you need for YKI, work, services, appointments, study and daily communication.

Start learning Finnish with Floently

Why Floently Finnish exists

Many learners need Finnish for serious life goals: passing YKI, working in Finland, applying for citizenship, communicating with employers, and handling everyday situations. Floently brings these needs into one learning path.

What you can practise

You can practise vocabulary, sentence patterns, grammar, roleplay, speaking situations, YKI-style tasks and workplace Finnish. The goal is to make Finnish usable, not just theoretical.

Who it is for

Floently Finnish is designed for immigrants, workers, students, healthcare professionals and adults who want structured Finnish practice for life in Finland.

Questions learners ask

What is Floently Finnish?

Floently Finnish is a Finnish learning app and web platform focused on YKI preparation, workplace Finnish, grammar, vocabulary, speaking practice and everyday Finnish.

Can Floently help me learn Finnish for work?

Yes. Floently includes workplace Finnish and roleplay-style practice for professional and everyday communication.

Is Floently only for beginners?

No. Floently is useful for learners who need practical Finnish for YKI, work and daily communication. It is especially useful when you already know some Finnish but need more confidence using it.

Floently Finnish is a learning and practice platform. It is not an official YKI test organizer and does not guarantee exam results.
